Malte ROLF, PhD
(formerly Malte Rolf-Pissarczyk)

Postdoctoral researcher

Institute of Applied Mechanics

FAU Erlangen-Nürnber, Germany

Since March 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Applied Mechanics at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg, Germany, working within Prof. Ellen Kuhl’s European Research Council Advanced Grant DISCOVER. I also continue to collaborate closely with the group of Prof. Gerhard A. Holzapfel at the Institute of Biomechanics at Graz University of Technology, Austria. This is where I completed my PhD in Biomedical Engineering with distinction in 2024, and I was fortunate to see my dissertation recognized with several national and international awards.

During my doctoral studies, I spent a year at the University of California, Berkeley, in 2016 and 2017 as a visiting researcher working with Prof. Panayiotis Papadopoulos, followed by a three-month research stay with Prof. Alison L. Marsden at Stanford University in 2022. Since then, I have remained connected to Stanford as a sponsored affiliate and currently return regularly for shorter visits to work with Prof. Ellen Kuhl.

research interests

  • Multiscale material modeling and model discovery for soft tissues
  • Modeling and simulation of diseased arteries, especially aortopathies
  • Growth and remodeling of arterial tissue and regenerative biomaterials
  • Verification, validation, and uncertainty quantification of in silico models
  • Surrogate-based uncertainty quantification with Bayesian inference
