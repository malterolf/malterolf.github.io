This joint project of Graz University of Technology and Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin, funded by the Austrian Science Fund (FWF) and the German Research Foundation (DFG) within the WEAVE programme, examines pathological changes in the carotid artery. The project is led by Prof. Gerhard A. Holzapfel and Prof. Leonid Goubergrits.

The project combines the characterization of diseased carotid tissue with computational modeling of wall mechanics and hemodynamics to better understand how pathological changes develop and how they can be assessed in individual patients. I contributed to the proposal as co-author and lead the computational contribution.
