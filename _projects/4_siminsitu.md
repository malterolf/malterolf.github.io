[SimInSitu](http://www.siminsitu.eu/) was a European Horizon 2020 project coordinated by 4RealSim Services. It developed an in silico platform for the development and clinical testing of heart valves made from biodegradable polymeric scaffolds, which are gradually replaced by the patient's own tissue.

At Graz University of Technology we characterized and modeled the anisotropic inelastic response of the polymeric scaffold materials and assessed the credibility of these material models. I co-authored the proposal and led one of the work packages.
