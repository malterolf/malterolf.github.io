[SIMCor](https://www.simcor-h2020.eu/) was a European Horizon 2020 project coordinated by Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin. Its goal was an open in silico platform for the development, validation and regulatory approval of cardiovascular implantable devices, including the generation and validation of virtual patient cohorts for in silico clinical trials.

At Graz University of Technology we contributed to the modeling of vascular tissue and to the validation strategy of the platform. I co-authored the proposal and led our contribution to the project.
