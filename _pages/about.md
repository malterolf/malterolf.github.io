I develop computational models of soft biological tissues and the methods to make them trustworthy. My work combines finite element simulations with Bayesian inference, surrogate modeling and machine learning, with a focus on arterial mechanics and aortic dissection.

I completed my doctorate with distinction at Graz University of Technology in 2024, working on the mechanics of aortic dissection with Gerhard A. Holzapfel. Since 2025 I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Institute of Applied Mechanics at Friedrich-Alexander-Universität Erlangen-Nürnberg.

Earlier publications appear under the name Malte Rolf-Pissarczyk.
